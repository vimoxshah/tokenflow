# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people learning
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when an individual
is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers
privately. Maintainers will review and respond as promptly as circumstances allow, and are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
